06 / KNOW YOUR POSITION

A price in context.

Five observed offers. Three brands. Different sports and coverage.

Competitive attractiveness ≠ financial viability.

The $49–$69 recommendation is a preliminary hypothesis. The $119.95 representative median includes different products; the sole direct clavicle benchmark is also $119.95 (n=1). Prices were observed October 27, 2026, are not live, exclude tax and delivery, and do not prove willingness to pay or equivalent protection.
